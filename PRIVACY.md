# GlobeMilo privacy policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026  
Publisher: **Lanray** (GitHub account: [lanray07](https://github.com/lanray07))  
Privacy and support contact: **banksmi@mail.com**

GlobeMilo is a geography learning game for children around 6–12 and their families. This policy describes the Android application and its optional spoken-guidance and Amazon purchase features.

## A short explanation for explorers

Your nickname and discoveries stay on your device. There are no adverts, public profiles or chats. Ask a grown-up before using the microphone. A grown-up can delete your explorer and its discoveries in the parent notebook.

## Local profiles and learning data

The app stores an explorer nickname, chosen avatar, difficulty, enabled activities, answers and attempts, completed journeys, dates and daily playtime on the device. These records support the games, adaptive difficulty, playtime controls and the parent progress view. Use a nickname rather than a child's full name.

GlobeMilo does not send these profiles or learning records to a publisher-operated server. There is no mandatory account, behavioural advertising, publisher analytics service, public leaderboard, social feed or open chat. The app does not request device location or access to contacts, photos, messages or calendars. App cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled in the application configuration.

## Spoken guidance

Optional narration uses the Android text-to-speech service installed on the device. It receives the instructions and geography text to speak. Voices may need a download or network access. Availability and processing depend on the installed provider. Touch controls remain available if a voice is missing or narration is turned off.

The app checks whether the Amazon Appstore (or Amazon App Tester during local testing) is installed and whether a compatible text-to-speech service is available, so it can enable the corresponding features. These are limited availability checks, not an upload of a general installed-app inventory.

## Microphone-free first release

Version 0.2.4 does not include microphone commands, request microphone permission, create a speech recognizer or record audio. Touch controls are available for every game. An earlier development build had optional push-to-talk; that feature is disabled in this release, including any opt-in saved by an earlier build.

Optional spoken guidance is text-to-speech output using the installed Android provider. It speaks game instructions and geography text, not a child's recordings. Voices may require downloads or network access; review the installed provider's terms and settings. The app does not guarantee every voice or language will be available.

The arithmetic parent gate is an interaction control. It is not a claim that legally verified parental consent has been obtained for other processing or every jurisdiction.

## Amazon purchases

Optional purchases and restoration use Amazon's Appstore SDK in the parent area. Amazon handles the purchase account, payment interface and transaction processing. GlobeMilo does not receive payment-card details.

The app receives Amazon product information, an app-specific Amazon user identifier and entitlement receipt information needed to handle purchases and restoration. It stores the Amazon account association and premium entitlement locally. Amazon and its Appstore services may process account, transaction and technical device information under their own privacy terms. See [Amazon's privacy notice](https://www.amazon.com/privacy). The publisher does not use purchase information for advertising or child profiling.

Deleting a local explorer does not cancel or erase an Amazon purchase or the purchaser's Amazon account. Purchase account and payment-data requests should be directed to Amazon.

## Retention and deletion

Local learning records remain until a parent deletes the relevant explorer, clears the app's storage or uninstalls it. Deleting an explorer removes its nickname, profile, attempts, journeys and associated local playtime records. Parent preferences and purchase access are kept separately when only one explorer is deleted.

For steps, read [data deletion instructions](DATA-DELETION.md). No publisher account needs to be deleted to remove local learning data. If a parent needs help, contact **banksmi@mail.com**. Do not send children's recordings, full names or unnecessary personal details in support requests.

## Support correspondence

If you voluntarily contact support, your email address and message are used to respond and address the request. Email delivery is handled by the relevant email providers. Correspondence should be retained only as needed to resolve the request or meet applicable recordkeeping requirements; it is separate from the game's local learning database.

## Parents and children's privacy

Parents control optional narration, difficulty and local profile deletion. Microphone commands are not included in this release. The game uses high-privacy defaults and does not require a child to provide a real name, birth date or email address. Optional third-party text-to-speech and purchase services have their own processing and retention practices. Parents can turn narration off and use the included games by touch.

Contact **banksmi@mail.com** for privacy questions or help exercising applicable access, correction or deletion rights. Because learning data is stored locally, the publisher cannot remotely retrieve or delete a device's learning database.
