# GlobeMilo support

Publisher: **Lanray**  
Customer support: **banksmi@mail.com**

Please contact us from a parent's email account. Include the app version, device model and a short description of the issue. Do not send children's recordings, full names or unnecessary personal information.

- **No voice?** Check narration in the parent area and the installed text-to-speech voice. All games also work by touch.
- **Microphone commands:** Version 0.3.0 adds free offline English commands. In the protected parent area, enable “Offline voice commands (English)” and allow microphone access. Return to a game or the home screen, tap the microphone, then say one of the displayed English phrases. Listening stops after at most eight seconds or on Cancel. Audio stays on the device and is not saved. All games work by touch. Earlier version 0.2.4 had no microphone commands.
- **Purchase unavailable?** A supported Amazon Appstore account, configured product and connectivity may be required. Try **Restore Amazon purchase** from the parent area if you already own the full collection. Never purchase again just to restore access.
- **Delete a profile?** Use the [data-deletion instructions](DATA-DELETION.md).
- **Privacy question?** Read the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md) or email support.

The app remains in release preparation. A public Amazon listing will be linked after publication.
